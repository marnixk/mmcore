name: MATH
kind: command
alias: CINT, EVAL, SINH, COSH, TANH, LOG10, ATAN3

CINT(n)
EVAL(expr$)
MATH(fn args)
MATH command  SET|SCALE|ADD|MUL|POWER|INTERPOLATE|
  SLICE|INSERT|M_*|V_*|Q_*|FFT

CINT rounds to nearest; .5 goes away from zero
(unlike INT=floor and FIX=truncate).
EVAL evaluates a string as an expression.
MATH() uses a space after the name, not extra
parentheses: MATH(ATAN3 x, y) not MATH(ATAN3(x,y)).

MATH(ATAN3 x, y)  angle of vector (x,y) in 0..2*PI
MATH(SINH a) MATH(COSH a) MATH(TANH a)
MATH(LOG10 a)
MATH(MAX a()) MATH(MIN a()) MATH(MEAN a())
MATH(MEDIAN a()) MATH(SUM a()) MATH(SD a())
MATH(MAGNITUDE v()) MATH(DOTPRODUCT a(), b())
MATH(M_DETERMINANT a())
MATH(CHI a()) MATH(CHI_P a()) MATH(CORREL a(), b())

MATH SET n, a()     fill every element
MATH SCALE a(), k, b()   or a(), b(), c()
MATH SCALE pts().x, k, pts().x
MATH ADD a(), k, b()     or a(), b(), c()
MATH MUL a(), b(), c()   element-wise (SCALE too)
MATH INTERPOLATE a(), b(), t, c()
MATH SLICE a(), i, , b()   (omit one index)
MATH INSERT a(), i, , b()
MATH M_INVERSE a(), b()    MATH M_TRANSPOSE a(), b()
MATH M_MULT a(), b(), c()  MATH M_PRINT a()
MATH V_PRINT a()  MATH V_NORMALISE a(), b()
MATH V_MULT m(), v(), o()  MATH V_CROSS a(), b(), c()
MATH Q_INVERT|Q_VECTOR|Q_EULER|Q_CREATE|Q_MULT|Q_ROTATE
MATH FFT a(), b()  (also INVERSE|MAGNITUDE|PHASE)

SD is sample stdev (n-1). Arrays are numeric.
TYPE arrays use arr().member the same way as
plain a() (PIXEL too; see TYPE).
OPTION ANGLE DEGREES applies to ATAN3 and Q_*.

Example:  PRINT CINT(45.57)
          PRINT EVAL("1+2*3")
          PRINT MATH(SUM A())
          MATH SCALE pos().x, 2, pos().x
