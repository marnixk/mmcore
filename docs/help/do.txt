name: DO
kind: language
alias: LOOP

Repeat a block of statements. The condition may be tested on DO and/or on LOOP.
With no test, the loop runs until EXIT DO (or BREAK). EXIT DO jumps to the
statement after LOOP.

DO [WHILE condition | UNTIL condition]
  statements
  EXIT DO
LOOP [WHILE condition | UNTIL condition]

  condition - expression; WHILE continues while non-zero, UNTIL until non-zero

Example:

    I = 0
    DO
      I = I + 1
      PRINT I
    LOOP UNTIL I = 3

    I = 0
    DO WHILE I < 3
      I = I + 1
      PRINT I
    LOOP

    DO
      IF INKEY$() <> "" THEN EXIT DO
    LOOP

    I = 0
    DO
      I = I + 1
    LOOP WHILE I < 3
