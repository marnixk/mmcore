name: DO
kind: language
alias: LOOP

DO [WHILE condition | UNTIL condition]
  statements
  EXIT DO
LOOP [WHILE condition | UNTIL condition]

Repeat a block. Test on DO and/or LOOP.
With no test, loops until EXIT DO (or BREAK).
EXIT DO jumps to the statement after LOOP.

Example:
  10 I=0
  20 DO
  30 I=I+1
  40 PRINT I
  50 LOOP UNTIL I=3
  RUN
