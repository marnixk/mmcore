name: PLAY
kind: command
alias: BEEP

Decode and play audio to OPTION AUDIO_TARGET. HDMI sends IEC958 stereo on
HDMI0. JACK sends PWM to the 3.5mm analogue socket (Pi 3 / Pi 4B). Pi 400 has
no analogue jack; use HDMI there. PLAYING() is 1 while audio is playing and
not paused.

PLAY STOP
PLAY RESET
PLAY PAUSE
PLAY RESUME
PLAY VOLUME left [, right]
PLAY TONE left, right [, duration]
PLAY MP3 file$
PLAY WAV file$
PLAY MODFILE file$     (PLAY MOD file$)
PLAY XM file$          (PLAY XMFILE file$)
PLAY S3M file$         (PLAY S3MFILE file$)
PLAY SFX file$ [, volume]
PLAY SFX VOLUME volume
PLAY SFX STOP

BEEP [frequency [, duration]]

  left, right - TONE frequencies in Hz, or VOLUME levels
  frequency - BEEP pitch in Hz (default 800)
  duration - TONE / BEEP length in milliseconds; for TONE omit to hold
             until PLAY STOP (BEEP defaults to 120)
  file$ - audio file path
  volume - 0..100 effect gain (default 100, set by PLAY SFX VOLUME)

On systems without audio output, files decode but no sound is heard; PLAYING()
reflects playback state.

About a third of a second of audio stays queued. The mixer also runs during
long screen updates, including a TERM repaint on another console.
MM.INFO("MIXGAP") is the longest gap between mixer services since PLAY
started, in milliseconds. MM.INFO("UNDERRUN") counts times the output queue
was empty when the mixer ran.

If other USB activity stalls the output device, the mixer restarts the
transfer automatically once data is queued again. PLAY RESET is an explicit
recovery if artifacts persist: it tears down and re-creates the output device
and clears its buffers, without stopping the current track or rebooting. A
playing track resumes in place from the mixer's current position.
MM.INFO("AUDIORESET") counts how many times PLAY RESET has run.

MOD, XM and S3M modules play through once and then stop (loops and jumps that
are part of the arrangement are honoured; the restart/order loop is not).
JUKE uses this to advance a folder queue to the next file.

MP3 files are streamed: PLAY reads the file in chunks and can start after a
small read-ahead, so a long track does not have to fit in RAM. MM.INFO("MP3BUF")
reports the peak bytes the decoder allocated. MP3s at 22.05, 32, 44.1 and
48 kHz are resampled to the 44100 Hz mixer, so duration and pitch match the
source; MM.INFO("MP3RATE"), "MP3SRC" and "MP3OUT" report the last file's source
rate and decoded/mixed frame counts.

Sound effects:

PLAY SFX adds a short WAV effect on top of the current music instead of
replacing it. Starting or ending an effect never stops, restarts, rewinds or
pauses the music, and it never blocks the program: the effect is mixed by the
audio service in the background, so the game loop keeps running. Effects also
play when no music is active; only their own output is sent.

Four effect voices mix at once. Triggering a fifth replaces the oldest one
still playing; retriggering the same file therefore overlaps with it until the
limit is reached. PLAY SFX STOP ends every effect and leaves the music alone.
PLAY STOP still stops everything. PLAY PAUSE pauses music and effects together,
and starting a new main track (PLAY MP3/WAV/MOD/XM/S3M/TONE) also ends any
effects because it replaces the shared engine.

Each voice is scaled by its own volume (the optional second argument, or the
PLAY SFX VOLUME default), and the music keeps its PLAY VOLUME gain, so the two
can be balanced independently. Music and effects are summed with saturation:
the mix clamps at full scale and never wraps around to the opposite sign.
MM.INFO("SFXVOICES") is the number of effects currently playing,
MM.INFO("SFXVOL") is the default effect gain, and MM.INFO("AUDIOPOS") is the
number of frames mixed for the current track; triggering or stopping an effect
does not change AUDIOPOS. PLAYING() still reports the main track only, so it
stays 1 while an effect plays over music and 0 when only an effect is active.

The mixer runs on every supported backend before PCM reaches the platform
sink, so concurrent effects behave the same on HDMI, the analogue jack and the
native SDL audio target. On a system with no audio device, effects decode and
mix but no sound is heard, the same as PLAY.

Example:

    OPTION AUDIO_TARGET HDMI
    PLAY WAV "tests/TEST.WAV"
    PLAY MP3 "tests/TEST.MP3"
    PLAY S3M "tests/TEST.S3M"
    PRINT PLAYING()

A game loop keeps a music track running and fires effects on events:

    OPTION AUDIO_TARGET HDMI
    PLAY MP3 "music/theme.mp3"
    PLAY SFX VOLUME 60
    DO
      IF KEYDOWN(32) THEN PLAY SFX "sound/jump.wav"
      IF KEYDOWN(13) THEN PLAY SFX "sound/coin.wav", 80
      ' ... update and draw the game ...
    LOOP

The same program is seeded as A:/apps/SFXTEST.BAS.
