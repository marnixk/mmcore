name: PLAY
kind: command

PLAY STOP
PLAY PAUSE
PLAY RESUME
PLAY VOLUME left [, right]
PLAY TONE left, right [, duration]
PLAY MP3 file$
PLAY WAV file$
PLAY MODFILE file$     (PLAY MOD file$)
PLAY XM file$          (PLAY XMFILE file$)

Decode and play audio to OPTION AUDIO_TARGET.
HDMI sends IEC958 stereo on HDMI0. JACK sends PWM
to the 3.5mm analogue socket (Pi 3 / Pi 4B). Pi 400
has no analogue jack; use HDMI there.
TONE frequencies are Hz; duration is milliseconds.
Omit duration to hold the tone until PLAY STOP.
PLAYING() is 1 while audio is playing and not paused.
Hardware keeps ~160ms in the DMA queue so HDMI/PWM
chunks are not padded with silence on a brief stall.
QEMU has no sound device; decode and PLAYING() still
run in real time.

Example:  OPTION AUDIO_TARGET HDMI
          PLAY WAV "TEST.WAV"
          PLAY MP3 "TEST.MP3"
          PRINT PLAYING()
