name: ARC
kind: command

ARC x, y, r1, r2, a1, a2 [, colour]

Elliptical arc centred at (x,y). r1 is the X radius,
r2 the Y radius. a1 and a2 are start and end angles
(radians, or degrees after OPTION ANGLE DEGREES).
Drawn as line segments.

Example:  OPTION ANGLE DEGREES
          ARC 160,120,40,40,0,90,RGB(255,255,0)
