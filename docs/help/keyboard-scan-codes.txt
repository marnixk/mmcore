Keyboard Scan Codes

PC/AT set-1 make codes (decimal). Useful with
~INKEY$~ extended keys and KEYDOWN.

  Esc 1          A 30           Caps Lock 58
  1 ! 2          S 31           F1 59
  2 @ 3          D 32           F2 60
  3 # 4          F 33           F3 61
  4 $ 5          G 34           F4 62
  5 % 6          H 35           F5 63
  6 ^ 7          J 36           F6 64
  7 & 8          K 37           F7 65
  8 * 9          L 38           F8 66
  9 ( 10         ; : 39         F9 67
  0 ) 11         ' " 40         F10 68
  - _ 12         ` ~~ 41         F11 133
  = + 13         Left Shift 42  F12 134
  Bksp 14        \ | 43         Num Lock 69
  Tab 15         Z 44           Scroll Lock 70
  Q 16           X 45           Home / 7 71
  W 17           C 46           Up / 8 72
  E 18           V 47           PgUp / 9 73
  R 19           B 48           Gray - 74
  T 20           N 49           Left / 4 75
  Y 21           M 50           Center / 5 76
  U 22           , < 51         Right / 6 77
  I 23           . > 52         Gray + 78
  O 24           / ? 53         End / 1 79
  P 25           Right Shift 54 Down / 2 80
  [ { 26         PrtSc / * 55   PgDn / 3 81
  ] } 27         Alt 56         Ins / 0 82
  Enter 28       Space 57       Del / . 83
  Ctrl 29

On this console, ~INKEY$~ maps VT100 arrows and
function keys to Colour Maximite 2 codes (Up=128).
See ~INKEY$~ for the mapped values.

Example:

    PRINT ASC(INKEY$)
