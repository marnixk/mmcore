name: RENAME
kind: command
alias: NAME, MV

RENAME src$ [AS|TO] dst$
NAME src$ AS dst$     (alias)
MV src$ [AS|TO] dst$  (alias)

Rename a file, or move it to another folder on the
same drive. Destination may be a new name or a path.

Example:  RENAME "B.TXT" AS "C.TXT"
          MV "C.TXT" TO "DATA/C.TXT"
