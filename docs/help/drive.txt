name: DRIVE
kind: command

List available drives, or select the current drive by letter.

DRIVE [letter$]

  letter$ - drive such as "A:" or "C:" (optional; omit to list drives)

A: is the RAM disk. C: is the SD card slot. D: and later are USB volumes when
present. B: is an internal package mount and is never listed.

Example:

    DRIVE
    DRIVE "A:"
