name: TRIM$
kind: command

Strip characters from the left, right or both ends of a string.

string$ = TRIM$(string$ [, mask$ [, where$]])

  mask$  - characters to remove (default a space)
  where$ - "L", "R" or "B" for left, right or both (default "B")

See also ~LTRIM$~, ~RTRIM$~.

Example:

    PRINT "[" + TRIM$("  hi  ") + "]"
